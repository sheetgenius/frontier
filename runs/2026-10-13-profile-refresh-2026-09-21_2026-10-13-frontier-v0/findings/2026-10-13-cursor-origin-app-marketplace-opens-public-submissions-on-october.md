# 2026-10-13-cursor-origin-app-marketplace-opens-public-submissions-on-october

Origin App Marketplace opens public submissions on October 13. Origin opens its marketplace to public app submissions. Apps can authenticate with installation tokens, receive webhooks and write checks, pull-request comments and reviews. A submission passes vendor review before listing. This is the third main-changelog entry in the window.

Channel: docs-only (dated hosted launch). Half: capability. Date: 2026-10-13.

Operator consequence: Origin becomes a broader integration destination for delivery and review tools. Review an app's installation permissions, delegation and uninstall path before enabling it. Marketplace listing is not proof that an integration has the scopes or operational behavior your team needs.
