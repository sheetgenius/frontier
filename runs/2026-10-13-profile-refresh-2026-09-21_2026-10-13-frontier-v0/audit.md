Authorities in order (THESIS, METHOD, EDITORIAL, AGENTS, the cycle and exemplar skills). ## What was decided

The run replaces each profile's posture rather than appending to it. Prior claims were re-checked: kept, narrowed, or retired in metadata, never stacked in the body.

Profiles lead with the version an operator can install today and its channel, then what changed that they must act on, then what is still preview, beta, flag-off or main-only. A main-only fix is reported as main-only even when it is the most interesting change of the window.

The six synthesis lenses agreed on the window's shape: agents took on more of the work around a coding task (background servers, native tool integration, programmable plan review, deployment monitoring), and the operator's attention moved to admitting processes, binding consent to the actual effect, and checking outcomes. That read is in SYNTHESIS.md with its receipts, with four connected stories proposed for the next weekly. It is not published here.

The posture audit removed a set-wide cautionary refrain from ten taglines and four headings. Severe facts stayed; the repeated sigh went. Capability gains (Pi's native MCP and tool scripting, OMP's programmable plan review, Cursor Rollouts, OpenHands automation drafts, Flue's approval recipe) are reported on the same receipt standard as breaks.

Live vendor documentation pages are cited through dated archive captures where a judge verified the capture, following the `official_docs` rule in CONTRIBUTING.md.

are taking over the handoffs people and private adapters used to perform
(composing tool calls, preparing recurring jobs, answering plan reviews), and
the operator's job moves to deciding which choices must still wait for a
person. The issue carries capability (Pi's native MCP and scripting, OpenHands
automation drafts, Temporal's durable script stepping, Cursor Rollouts and
Origin) on the same receipt standard as the failures (Eve's consent timing,
OpenClaw hook arguments, the OpenHands terminal key, Claude mods answering
permission checks).

Evidence that cuts against the thesis is kept: rishi's plan-mode reply
against the claim that better models retire the planning pause; Shantanu Goel
and Mario Zechner against native batching as a free win; the Unreal comparison
left unsettled rather than dismissed. One correction to "Before the First Turn"
is printed in the issue.

Uncertain and not settled here: every OpenHands, Pi, Eve and Temporal runtime
path is source-derived; no trial or replay was run. Vendor and practitioner
posts are reported as statements; no claimed saving or team practice was
audited.
