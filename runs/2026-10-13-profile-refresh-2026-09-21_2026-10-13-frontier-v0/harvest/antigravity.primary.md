## 17. Rate-limit retries follow the server delay and stop on daily or billing caps from 1.2.13

- **Date:** 2026-09-29
- **Channel:** `tagged-release`

- **Half:** both | **Confidence:** high (vendor-stated)

**What changed.** The CLI replaced a fixed five-second rate-limit retry with the server-requested delay. It stops immediately if that delay exceeds 30 seconds or the quota is a daily or billing cap, rather than repeatedly trying a request the account cannot serve.

## 19. Resuming a history with a missing step stopped overwriting its newest messages in 1.2.14
