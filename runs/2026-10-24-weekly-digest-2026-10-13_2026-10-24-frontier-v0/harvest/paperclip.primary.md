**What changed.** Filesystem receipts use exclusive creation, restricted permissions, sync and rename; reconciliation retains receipts until the database transaction commits. Run settlement uses a stable idempotency key, records ledger/runtime acknowledgment together and cleans receipts after commit. Company-row locking serializes accounting and admission reservations. Exact fractional cents and reported/estimated/unpriced states replace invented zero charges. **What changed.** Hiring, approval, preparation, verification, pause and termination now share a durable lifecycle owner. Approved hires must finish required setup and harness verification before work; stop/cleanup evidence gates completion, with persisted retries and visible pending phases. Persistent identity can replace bespoke per-run signing setup, but recipients must decide trust and revocation, and the host remains trusted with the key.
