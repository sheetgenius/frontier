# Michael Ruescher

Michael edits Bitter Frontier. He sets the watchlist, decides what a cycle
covers, and owns every editorial call in it.

The production method is unusual and worth stating plainly, because it is also
this publication's thesis in practice. The research behind each issue is
executed by the same class of coding agents the issues cover, running sweeps,
cross-checks, and drafts under his direction, with the full run trails published
in the open. The agents do the reading. The judgment of what reaches a page is
his, and so is the record when that judgment was wrong: the
[corrections ledger](/corrections/) is signed by the editor, not by the
machinery.

If this publication quoted you and got it wrong, or you want a quotation of
yours removed, the fastest route is [@ruemic](https://x.com/ruemic) or an issue
on the repository. The standing promise is in CONTRIBUTING.md: misquotations are
fixed fast, removal requests are honored, and disagreements get argued in the
open rather than quietly edited away.
