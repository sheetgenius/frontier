# The Owner Can Be an Agent

[Cursor Origin, Cursor's early-beta code-hosting service](https://web.archive.org/web/20261024092716/https://cursor.com/docs/origin), [can accept its agent's ownership decision in place of the code-owner approval count in Agentic mode](https://web.archive.org/web/20261024094000id_/https://cursor.com/docs/release-notes/origin.md). Origin can therefore clear the code-owner gate without a human owner's approval; teams must choose which paths may work that way.

**Issue window: 13-24 October 2026 (UTC).** The 13 October label marks the previous issue's cutoff. This 11-day catch-up covers everything after 13 October, 23:59:59 UTC, through the 24 October source reads. Origin's ownership rules describe its current beta; [the release note does not establish that they launched during this window](https://web.archive.org/web/20261024094000id_/https://cursor.com/docs/release-notes/origin.md).

## Code review can carry the owner's authority

For Origin teams, the code-owner setting decides who can clear the review gate. It offers [three modes: Off; Classic, where an owner must approve the files they own; and Agentic](https://web.archive.org/web/20261024094000id_/https://cursor.com/docs/release-notes/origin.md). An [installed app named in `ORIGIN_CODEOWNERS` can also approve the paths it owns, including under Classic](https://web.archive.org/web/20261024094000id_/https://cursor.com/docs/release-notes/origin.md). Yannis asks for narrower worker authority:

<!--card:yannis-origin-per-agent-authority-->

Before wider delegation, require documentation stating when agent decisions replace owner approval, inspectable approval records and tests showing whether an app can approve its own patch.

Claude's hosted code-review service leaves approval to others. ClaudeDevs announced [[q:claudedevs-code-review-ga-depth]] The [15 October announcement](https://web.archive.org/web/20261016015421id_/https://claude.dev/blog/solving-code-review-at-anthropic/) and service contract describe [findings and a check, without the service approving or blocking the PR](https://web.archive.org/web/20261017030656/https://code.claude.com/docs/en/code-review). For Claude, count useful findings. For Origin, count owner decisions avoided and overturned.

Where a required check must actually run, test whether a skipped or neutral result lets a merge through. Origin [runs agents in CI prompt steps in team-owned namespaces](https://web.archive.org/web/20261024094000id_/https://cursor.com/docs/release-notes/origin.md), while its [required-check rule accepts neutral and skipped conclusions](https://web.archive.org/web/20261024094330id_/https://cursor.com/docs/api/origin/reference/create-ruleset.md). An agent deciding that the work does not apply can therefore satisfy the gate. Where that is unacceptable, require a separate check that can pass only by running.

For Claude tool calls needing human approval, verify that the host callback reaches a person. Claude [2.1.304 adds `decider: "person"`](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases/tag/v2.1.304), which selects who may approve the call. Ayyaz describes the boundary: [[q:ayyaz-person-decider-host-boundary]]

Netlify describes its [documented beta integration](https://web.archive.org/web/20261001190400id_/https://www.netlify.com/blog/cursor-origin-repositories-on-netlify/): [[q:netlify-origin-beta-pipeline]] Cynthia Toporowski argues for a separate forge: [[q:cynthia-open-forge-counter-read]] Before moving a repository to Origin, decide who should hold it and what switching away would cost.

## Native tools give builders machinery to retire

Pi adapter users have a second interpreter to reconsider. On 22 October, optional [pi-mcp-adapter 6.0.0 removed QuickJS, its separate script tools and scripting skill in favor of Pi's codemode](https://github.com/nicobailon/pi-mcp-adapter/blob/v6.0.0/CHANGELOG.md), Pi's native scripting tool. Before maintaining a separate interpreter for external-tool calls, test whether Pi's native scripting tool covers the same work.

Permission checks can now see which tool a script actually calls. The adapter [exposes real deferred MCP tool identities to permission hooks](https://github.com/nicobailon/pi-mcp-adapter/blob/v6.0.0/CHANGELOG.md#L27). For the supported integration with Executor app tools, [when the adapter recognizes an approval request, Pi asks in a dialog; the model cannot approve, and the call fails without a confirmation UI](https://github.com/nicobailon/pi-mcp-adapter/blob/v6.0.0/CHANGELOG.md#L26).

Gemini CLI [stable 0.65.0 leaves referenced directories for the agent to explore instead of eagerly reading their files](https://github.com/google-gemini/gemini-cli/blob/v0.65.0/packages/cli/src/ui/hooks/atCommandProcessor.ts#L535-L549). A directory can become a place to investigate instead of a sack of text to stuff into context. Name indispensable files explicitly while testing whether your directory-packing wrapper still earns its keep.

Claude [2.1.302 enables forks on specified headless and SDK paths](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases/tag/v2.1.302), and [2.1.305 reuses completed `pipeline()` and `parallel()` agents from the workflow journal](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases/tag/v2.1.305). ## A continuing job needs a recovery owner

Codex repairs conversation recovery: [0.164.0 records compaction summaries only after completion](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/rust-v0.164.0/codex-rs/core/src/compact.rs#L858-L890) and [restores compressed histories](https://github.com/openai/codex/commit/76ed3b484d62ccceb9fa512ff4dd139f421814c3). ## A machine owner is a policy choice

[Bitter Lesson Maxing](/bitter-lesson/) means building where improving general agents compound your advantage. Retire prompt-copy, interpreter and restart code when the supported replacement preserves your application's contracts. Keep the procedures, accounting records and recovery code that still cover work the general loop leaves undone.

[Amdahl Maxing](/amdahls-law/) means spending scarce human attention where judgment changes the outcome. Thariq puts the question to anyone running several agents at once: [[q:thariq-one-p0]] Keep the task and acceptance test fixed. Judge delegation by accepted results per hour of human intervention, counting recovery, overturned decisions, report reading and migration.

Choose the Origin paths where an agent's ownership decision can stand without routine human review. Keep a named person responsible for exceptions and reversals.
